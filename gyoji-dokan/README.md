# 行持道環 Gyōji-dōkan

Interactive diagram illustrating Dōgen's concept of continuous practice from *Shōbōgenzō* (正法眼蔵).

## Original Text

> 「仏祖の大道、かならず無上の行持あり、道環して断絶せず。発心・修行・菩提・涅槃、しばらく間隙あらず、行持道環なり」
>
> "The great Way of Buddha ancestors always has supreme continuous practice. It circles as the Way, never breaking off. Aspiration, practice, awakening, nirvana—not a moment's gap between them."

> 「いまの華開葉落、これ行持の現成なり。磨鏡破鏡、それ行持にあらざるなし。」
>
> "The flower blooming, the leaf falling—this is gyōji manifesting. Polishing the mirror, shattering the mirror—none of this is apart from gyōji."
>
> — Dōgen, *Shōbōgenzō*, Gyōji (c. 1242)

## Key Insight

The four (発心・修行・菩提・涅槃) are **not sequential stages** on a path. They exist **simultaneously**, with no gap between them. Practice itself is enlightenment (修証一等 *shushō-ittō*).

This diagram follows the interpretation found in Sen'e's commentary *Kikigaki-shō* (聞書抄), a record by Dōgen's disciple: the four are not stages that cycle, but aspects of a single reality that arise together in every moment of practice.

## Breaking Down the Terms

### 行持 (Gyōji) — Continuous Practice

| Kanji | Reading | Meaning |
|-------|---------|---------|
| 行 | gyō | practice, action |
| 持 | ji | sustain, maintain |

Sustained daily practice. Not just meditation, but every act of daily life: waking, eating, cleaning, working. The foundation that carries everything.

### 道環 (Dōkan) — The Way Circles

| Kanji | Reading | Meaning |
|-------|---------|---------|
| 道 | dō | the Way, path |
| 環 | kan | ring, circle |

The Way forms a ring with no endpoint. There is no "completion" or "arrival"—practice continues endlessly.

### The Four

| Japanese | Reading | English | Meaning |
|----------|---------|---------|---------|
| 発心 | hosshin | Aspiration | The arising of the mind that seeks the Way |
| 修行 | shugyō | Practice | Training, discipline—for Dōgen, practice IS enlightenment |
| 菩提 | bodai | Awakening | Enlightenment (Sanskrit: bodhi) |
| 涅槃 | nehan | Nirvana | Liberation, peace beyond suffering (Sanskrit: nirvāṇa) |

### 華開葉落 / 磨鏡破鏡

| Japanese | Reading | Meaning |
|----------|---------|---------|
| 華開葉落 | kakai yōraku | Flowers bloom, leaves fall—the ordinary is practice |
| 磨鏡破鏡 | makyō hakyō | Polish the mirror (cultivate), shatter the mirror (release even that) |

## Diagram Structure

- **Large outer sphere** — The whole, encompassing everything as one unified reality
- **Four spheres on the torus** — 発心, 修行, 菩提, 涅槃 (overlapping, simultaneous)
- **Torus (ring) base** — 行持, the foundation of continuous daily practice
