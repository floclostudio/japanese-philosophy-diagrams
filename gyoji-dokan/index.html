行持 道環

Gyōji-dōkan

「仏祖の大道、かならず無上の行持あり、
道環して断絶せず。
発心・修行・菩提・涅槃、
しばらく間隙あらず、行持道環なり」

"The great Way of Buddha ancestors always has supreme continuous practice.
It circles as the Way, never breaking off.
Aspiration, practice, awakening, nirvana—
not a moment's gap between them."

— Dōgen, Shōbōgenzō, Gyōji (c. 1242)
